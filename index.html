📡 GlassCall Pro

Secure peer-to-peer calls • No account needed

How to use:

  1. Your 6-digit Room Key is shown on the top. Share it with your friend.
  2. Friend opens the same page and enters your key (or you enter their key).
  3. Choose Voice Call or Video Call.
  4. When someone calls you, Accept or Decline.
  5. During call you can:
    • Mute / Unmute mic
    • Turn camera on/off (video calls)
    • Add more people with ➕
    • Share your screen
    • Chat at the bottom

Tip: Both people must keep this page open. Best on Chrome or Firefox.
